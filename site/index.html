Questions people ask

Is CSD registration free?

Yes. Registering on the CSD is free and you can do it yourself. My fee is for helping you do it properly, the first time.

Do you promise I’ll win a tender?

No. Nobody honest can. Tenders are public and free, and you bid yourself, the normal way. I help you get ready and find the right ones. See our terms and privacy notice.

Do I give you my passwords?

No. You log in with your own details and I guide you. Your documents stay private.

Where do the tenders come from?

National Treasury’s eTenders portal and the Gauteng e-Tender portal, checked every morning. Some municipalities and state companies put a few tenders only on their own websites; we don’t have those yet.

What does “compulsory briefing” mean?

The buyer holds a meeting or site visit before the closing date, and only companies that attended may bid. We show the date and time when the documents state it. Always check the documents too.

What happens after the 14 days?

We send one email to say the trial is over. Nothing is charged. To keep getting tenders, it’s R149 a month or R1,490 a year through PayFast.

Can I change my trade or provinces?

Yes. Sign up again with the same email address and pick again. Your trial still counts from your first sign-up.
