Frequently asked questions

Isn't CSD registration free?

Yes. Registering on the CSD with National Treasury is free, and you can do it yourself. Our fee is for doing it with you, checking your details are correct, and fixing problems so your registration doesn't get stuck.

Will you help me win a tender?

We help you submit complete, correct, on-time bids, which is where most small businesses lose out. But nobody can guarantee a win, and anyone who promises that is not being honest with you.

What documents do I need to start?

Usually your CIPC company registration, director ID copies, bank confirmation letter and SARS details. We send you an exact checklist after your first message.

How fast is it?

Most registrations are done in a few working days. Some steps depend on SARS, CIPC or CIDB. Need it urgently? Add Express 48-hour for R200 and we prioritise your work.

How do I pay?

By EFT. We send our banking details on WhatsApp or email. Registrations and packs are paid upfront. Bid Help is 50% upfront and 50% before submission.

Do you work outside Gauteng?

Yes. Everything is done online and on WhatsApp, so we can help businesses anywhere in South Africa.

Can I cancel alerts?

Yes, anytime. No contracts.
